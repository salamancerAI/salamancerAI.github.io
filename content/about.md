SalamancerAI is a pen name. This blog is a learning journal: notes on making things with AI tools, including what I tried, what worked, what didn't, and what I think it means. It isn't a set of tutorials or expert opinion. Where I'm guessing, I say so.

## How the writing is produced

Human-directed, AI-assisted. I choose the subjects, run the experiments, and decide what to publish. I draft with AI assistance and then edit by hand. Images, where used, are AI-generated and chosen by me.

## What to expect

Honest write-ups of small creative and technical experiments, failures included, with specific questions at the end when I'm stuck. Corrections and suggestions are welcome.

Corrections and suggestions via GitHub Issues: [github.com/salamancerAI/salamancerAI.github.io/issues](https://github.com/salamancerAI/salamancerAI.github.io/issues)
